Welcome!

I am an applied economist with research interests in labor economics, the economics of education, applied microeconomics, and public policy. I received my Ph.D. in Economics from the University of California, Riverside, in 2026.  

My research focuses on applying modern econometric methods and causal inference techniques to large-scale administrative data to evaluate policies and examine their impacts on individuals, institutions, and society. More broadly, I am interested in producing rigorous empirical evidence to inform policy design and decision-making.
